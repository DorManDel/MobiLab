EXPERIMENT 03 / VIBRATION PHYSICS

VibroMotion

Can phone vibration cause real movement? Run short, repeatable trials, record angular change and motion sensor signals, and annotate what you actually see.

!
Safety first. Flat tests need a padded, enclosed tray away from edges. Upright trials require a stable padded jig that cannot tip over. No open-table upright experiments. This app cannot prevent a physical fall.
